# Captain Tommy Website Mission Directive

Date: 2026-04-15
Project: `captain-tommy-website`

## Core mission

Build `captain-tommy-website` as a personal, evolving, adventure-driven home on the web for Captain Tommy.

This is **not** primarily a recruiter site and **not** an influencer site.

The homepage should feel like:
- a life underway
- a travel-and-story archive in progress
- a quietly cool personal brand
- a place that friends, family, interesting strangers, and potential employers can all browse without it feeling like a résumé exploded on landing

The CV/recruiter path should remain easy to access, but it should live as a **subpage / side door**, not dominate the main experience.

## Tone and positioning

Target tone:
- wry
- competent
- adventurous
- grounded
- quietly confident
- not flashy
- not preachy
- not self-mythologizing

The site should communicate:
- Tommy has actually lived an unusual and interesting life
- Tommy knows what he is doing
- Tommy steps outside the comfort zone, but not blindly
- Tommy is not trying to look famous
- Tommy is not trying to sell a fantasy
- Tommy is simply leaving the trail visible
- Tommy is calm under pressure and quietly funny without becoming performative
- Tommy notices the human character of places, not just the scenery
- Tommy is deeply responsible for the people, vessel, and experience under his care

## Critical guardrails

Do **not** make Tommy look like:
- a reckless moron
- a fake adventurer
- an influencer cliché
- a self-important sea philosopher
- a generic “luxury lifestyle” salesman
- someone glamorizing dangerous decisions for attention

If there is tension between “cool” and “credible,” choose **credible**.
If there is tension between “dramatic” and “true,” choose **true**.
If there is tension between “slick” and “human,” choose **human**.

## Narrative frame

Best current framing:
- People call it luck, but luck tends to appear in places where uncertainty is real.
- Tommy tends to walk through doors that open rather than obsess over what might be on the other side.
- That openness is not random drifting. It is calculated risk, intentional movement, and a willingness to leave the comfort zone.
- He is not surrendering to chaos or “wherever the tide decides.” He is choosing motion over stagnation while staying credible and capable.
- His life is not “better than normal,” just less standard issue.
- He is exposing the trail, not pushing a story.

Useful thematic language:
- life underway
- the trail visible
- voyages, boats, miles, and stories
- doors that opened
- places where luck matters
- hard-earned calm
- calculated openness
- motion over stagnation
- not a blueprint, just the path taken
- every port has a character
- a good run looks easy from the outside
- more than driving the boat
- built in engine rooms, wheelhouses, marinas, anchorages, and shipyards

## Homepage job

The homepage should:
1. establish Captain Tommy’s vibe and worldview
2. create curiosity and emotional texture
3. show the life, not just qualifications
4. support ongoing additions of photos, logs, passages, ports, and seasonal chapters
5. make the visitor want to explore further

The homepage should **not** read like:
- a hiring funnel first
- a LinkedIn profile with fancier fonts
- a brag wall

## CV page job

`cv.html` should remain the recruiter/employer-friendly page for:
- credentials
- work history
- contact info
- downloadable PDFs
- QR-code handoff

That page can remain clean, practical, and direct.

## Content lanes to build over time

Preferred recurring content lanes:
- Passages
- Ports
- Boats
- Captain’s Log
- Seasonal chapters
- Views worth earning
- Lessons from the wheelhouse
- The current chapter
- Places worth returning to
- Boats brought into shape
- Human moments tied to place

## Photo strategy

Photos are expected to become a major part of the site’s real identity.

Use the photo library to extract:
- strong hero candidates
- narrative-supporting voyage photos
- visually varied region images
- lifestyle/atmosphere moments
- photos that make the life feel real rather than staged

Prioritize images that signal:
- competence
- place
- movement
- earned beauty
- actual lived experience

Avoid overusing:
- cheesy luxury shots
- generic marina filler
- anything that feels try-hard
- anything that unintentionally suggests irresponsibility

## Interaction strategy

Interactive elements should feel tasteful and story-driven, not gimmicky.

Strong directions:
- route-map / travel trail
- Captain’s Log timeline
- polaroid/photo-stack browsing
- seasonal chapter browsing
- story cards tied to places or boats
- place + chapter + image/story hook cards that can grow over time

Avoid novelty for novelty’s sake.

## Decision rule for future sessions

When making design or copy choices, ask:
1. Does this feel like Tommy, not a template?
2. Does this increase story/adventure/personal texture?
3. Does this preserve credibility and competence?
4. Does this help the site keep growing after the current job search?
5. Would this still feel good if viewed by friends/family, a random dockside contact, or a serious employer?

If the answer to several of those is “no,” don’t ship it.
