# MISSION DIRECTIVE - NORTH STAR

This project is not just a website.

It is an autobiographical system for telling the truth about Captain Tommy's life, work, judgment, movement, and way of solving problems.

It should help people understand who Tommy is, what he values, how he thinks, and what kind of life he has actually lived, without flattening that life into generic brochure copy, influencer sludge, or recruiter-first polish.

## Core purpose

The site should let people feel the shape of Tommy's world:
- the miles
- the work
- the places
- the patterns
- the pressure
- the memories
- the humor
- the competence
- the standards
- the problem-solving instinct

This is not a decorative travel diary.
This is not a sterile CV site.
This is not a luxury-brand fantasy.

It is a living, navigable autobiography built from real routes, real work, real stories, and real voice.

## Primary product truth

The most important layer is the intake layer.

If Tommy can send rough material from real life, especially while underway or in port, and the system can reliably turn it into structured content that still sounds like him, the project becomes real.

If intake is weak, every future post becomes manual rescue.
If intake is strong, the site can grow naturally from real life.

So when priorities compete, prefer:
1. intake and voice integrity
2. content truth and usability
3. presentation polish
4. decorative cleanup

## Voice truth

Tommy's writing voice is not optional decoration.
It is part of the substance.

When Tommy sends longer prose in his own voice, treat that as both:
- content input
- voice evidence

When Tommy sends fragments, rough notes, short logs, or compressed memories:
- consult `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` before expanding
- shape the material so it sounds more like Tommy, not more like an assistant
- preserve grounded specifics, operational texture, dry understatement, and lived detail
- avoid sounding macho, corporate, over-literary, fake-poetic, or generically polished

When new recurring voice patterns appear in Tommy's own writing, update `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` so the model improves over time.

## Experience goal

The Travel Trail and broader site should be one of a kind, but not confusing.

The experience should work for:
- a 94-year-old grandmother
- a technically fluent friend
- a potential employer
- a curious stranger

That means the interface must make complex information feel simple without making it shallow.
The heavy lifting belongs behind the scenes.
Clarity on the surface, depth underneath.

## Guardrails

Do not let the project drift into:
- generic yachting luxury branding
- empty cinematic mood without substance
- recruiter-first flattening
- overbuilt information architecture that stops real posting
- clever interaction that makes normal browsing harder
- assistant voice replacing Tommy voice

## Definition of success

The project is succeeding when:
- Tommy can send material quickly from sea or from port
- that material can be turned into logs, stories, notes, and memories without losing his voice
- the site makes his world easy to enter and rewarding to explore
- people come away with a better sense of who he is and how he moves through the world
- the system feels elegant, alive, and durable instead of fragile and hand-maintained

4. Use that real content to tune the presentation layer
5. Continue refining Travel Trail into the clearest, richest way to explore Tommy's world

When in doubt, return to this question:

**Does this make the project a truer, more usable expression of Tommy's real life and voice, or is it just making the site look busier?**
