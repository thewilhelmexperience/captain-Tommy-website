Running the coast right now.

Currently underway on a delivery from West Palm Beach to Northern New Jersey — a route I know well. The ICW through Georgia and the Carolinas, offshore windows timed around the weather, a Chesapeake Bay run if the forecast cooperates north of Hatteras.

This kind of delivery focuses the mind. It’s just the vessel, the passage plan, and the decisions that need to be made as conditions develop. No drama, no improvising — just the methodical execution of a plan built on experience and good weather routing.
