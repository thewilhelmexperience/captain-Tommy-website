This is a seed example note entry.

A note like this exists for the kind of truth that is too useful to lose and too small to force into a full story. The crossing never cares what the calendar says. What matters is whether the day feels right, whether the window is real, and whether the sea state matches the optimistic story people tell themselves at the dock.

In the archive model, a note like this should be able to sit under the crossing place, connect to a log entry, and attach to one or more supporting images. That makes it useful both as a piece of seamanship memory and as part of the broader narrative graph.
