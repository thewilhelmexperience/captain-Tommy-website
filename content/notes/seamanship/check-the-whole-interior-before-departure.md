Never depart the dock, no matter how short the trip is, without checking the entire interior, even if you know you were the last person in those spaces.

An owner bought a new tender and it was at the dealership's service slip, and I was sent to move it to the owner's house. It was just a short smooth water run in a new boat. I went through all my mechanical checks, did a quick deck sweep, and staged lines and fenders for a quick landing on arrival.

Apparently the owner's kids had put together a congratulations gift in the V berth, a serving tray on the foot of the bed with several bottles of red wine and crystal stemware. None of it made sense as something the kids would ever actually use or probably ever even see in the tender V berth, but they did it anyway. The surprise was found by the crew smashed on the floor with red wine painting the lower sections of the walls and shards of high end wine glass everywhere.

So I never cast off without checking the whole interior.
