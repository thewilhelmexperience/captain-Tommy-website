Every time I pull into Compass Cay, the **Welcome Home** sign gets me.

It is weathered, hand-painted, and sitting on the starboard side as you enter the harbor. After you work your way around the entrance channel and round that last corner, there it is waiting for you as you come in.

Maybe that is part of why it lands so well. You do not just happen to notice it. You earn the sight of it a little.

And every time I see it, it makes my heart feel happy.

Part of that is the place itself, but a bigger part is the people. The guys there have a way of making you feel like you are part of their home, and they do it genuinely, not as a performance and not as some polished act meant to keep people coming back. It feels real. That is the difference.

There are plenty of beautiful stops in the Bahamas, and plenty of places that know how to be charming. Compass Cay stays with me because it feels welcoming in a deeper way than that. It does not feel like you are arriving at a destination. It feels like you are being received.

That is why it has become one of my favorite stops overall.
