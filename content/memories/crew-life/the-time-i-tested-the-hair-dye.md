Near the end of a three-month trip, the boss’s boyfriend was due to join the boat for the last couple of weeks. That meant preparations, and among them was one very specific problem: her hairstylist had sent her along with the proper coloring so she could find a local salon and get touched up before he arrived.

Only there was no local hairdresser available where we were, and he was arriving the next day.

So the plan changed. The stew was going to do it.

That was fine right up until it became real. Just before they started, the boss began questioning whether this was actually a good idea. I happened to be walking through the galley while that discussion was going on, and I could feel the uncertainty building. So I offered the only solution that seemed useful at the time.

The stew could use me as the practice canvas first.

If she did a terrible job, I could always shave my head. The boss, understandably, did not have that option.

She looked slightly stunned that I would volunteer for this, but she accepted. So there I was, seated for an unofficial hair-color trial, reading the instructions from the professional while the stew more or less ignored them and did it her own way. I was not the best patient, mostly because I could see in real time that “her way” and “the actual directions” were not especially aligned.

Still, in the end, she did a good job. The boss was reassured, the real coloring went ahead, and she looked good for the boyfriend’s arrival.

My hair, on the other hand, came out looking like **The Donald**.

At first it was just funny and a little absurd. But the best part came later, after we got back to the States. By then I had mostly adapted to it, or maybe just stopped noticing. Then I went to my usual barber, and she started laughing the second she saw me. Fortunately for me, part of her training was fixing bad dye jobs, so she managed to make me look more normal again. Even then, it took a long time before the last of it finally grew out.

For a while I looked like a man with an unhealthy devotion to the boss’s exact hair color. It solved the problem, though, which is why I still think it was worth it.
