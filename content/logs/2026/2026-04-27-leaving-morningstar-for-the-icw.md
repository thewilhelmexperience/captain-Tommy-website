We were off the dock from Morningstar at 6:00 this morning and started up the Cape Fear River to begin the ICW portion of the trip.

By 6:30 we were heading up the river properly, with yesterday's offshore plan now behind us and the run shifting inland instead.

The main thing worth noting at departure was how dramatically the weather had changed. It was 55 degrees with a 15-knot northeast wind, which gave the morning a very different feel from the recent South Florida starts.

That changes the feel of the day. After the weather forced the stop in Southport, this morning starts as a more deliberate inside leg, pointed north again and back to the practical work of moving the boat up the line.

Some days do not go exactly according to plan, and this one turned into that kind of day. A mechanical issue slowed us down and forced us to stop in Beaufort, North Carolina instead of making the longer push we had in mind.

Once that happened, the focus shifted away from keeping up with the log and onto finding the quickest clean resolution. Between local recommendations and some internet digging, we were able to get the problem diagnosed and the failed parts removed before the end of the day, which was about as good an outcome as we could have hoped for once things started going sideways.

Service is lined up for first thing in the morning, so the delay looks manageable instead of open-ended. That is the part worth appreciating. Mechanical problems are part of being at sea, and in a different place this could easily have turned into a much longer and more frustrating layover.

Instead, Beaufort turned out to be a pretty good place to get stuck for a day. The interruption was unexpected, but it looks like we should be able to get moving again quickly, and there are worse places to spend an extra day while the boat gets put back in order.

That extra day alongside gives us room to handle repairs properly, catch up on systems maintenance, and do some cleaning so the rest of the run goes more smoothly once we are moving again.
