I left Palm Harbor Marina this morning on a northbound delivery and made the 6:45 bridge opening to get out Lake Worth Inlet right at sunrise.

That part of the day had a good look to it, even if it was not exactly a dramatic Florida postcard morning. Everything was a little grey and overcast, but the light coming up over the inlet still gave the run a proper start. Once outside, I turned north and settled in on the ocean with St. Augustine as the goal for the day.

It has been an easy ride so far. The sky has stayed dull, but the following seas have kept things smooth, which is a trade I will take every time. There is not much to complain about when the boat is moving well, the inlet is behind you, and the day opens up in front of you one clean leg at a time.

For now this is the start of another northbound string, with today aimed at St. Augustine and the rest of the trip to follow from there.
