I took command of the boat less than 24 hours before departure from Florida and had to learn it all the way up the coast. A few low sleep days into my command we were passing under the Verrazzano Bridge into lower New York Harbor. I had finally repaired the radar and plotter, and it was the first nice June weekend day in New York, so when I entered the harbor the spiderweb of target tracks that appeared on the screen made for a distinct change in operation from the offshore Atlantic run of the previous few days.
