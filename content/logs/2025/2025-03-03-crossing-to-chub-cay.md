This is a seed example log entry, meant to prove the content model rather than claim a final polished narrative.

Left South Florida on a clean enough weather window and settled into the crossing rhythm, the kind where the job becomes less about talking and more about paying attention. The point of a log like this in the system is not just to hold prose, but to pin a dated movement to real places, a real chapter arc, and supporting images that can back up the memory later.

As the run moved east, the crossing stopped being an abstract line on a map and became the emotional hinge between familiar water and island water. That is exactly the kind of transition the archive should be able to show, not only in photos, but in written material that can appear on the homepage as a recent log and still live permanently inside the full Travel Trail archive.

Arrival at Chub Cay gives the entry a clear destination anchor, but the important structural point is that this one log can relate to Fort Lauderdale, the crossing itself, Chub Cay, the Exploration Years chapter, and multiple images without duplicating anything.
